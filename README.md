# Retail Sales Performance Analysis

End-to-end retail sales analysis using **SQL, MySQL, Python, and Power BI** to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Superstore Sales Dataset (2014–2017)** to evaluate sales performance, profitability, products, customers, regions, states, and shipping methods.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow:

**Data Cleaning → SQL Analysis → Python EDA → Power BI Dashboards → Business Insights → Recommendations**

## 🎯 Objectives

- Clean and validate retail sales data
- Perform business analysis using SQL and MySQL
- Conduct Exploratory Data Analysis using Python
- Create meaningful visualizations
- Build interactive Power BI dashboards
- Identify profitable and loss-making areas
- Generate data-driven business recommendations

## 📊 Dataset

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset  
**Analysis Period:** 2014–2017  
**Records:** 9,994  
**Business Domain:** Retail

Key fields include:

`Sales, Profit, Quantity, Discount, Customer, Product, Category, Region, State, Segment, Ship Mode, Order Date`

## 📈 Power BI Dashboards

### 1. Executive Dashboard
Provides a high-level view of overall sales, profit, customers, orders, category performance, regional performance, and yearly trends.

### 2. Product & Customer Analysis
Analyzes best-selling products, profitable products, loss-making products, top customers, and shipping performance.

### 3. Business Insights
Provides deeper analysis of state-level sales, regional profitability, category margins, shipping performance, and loss-making territories.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Revenue increased from approximately **$484.2K in 2014 to $733.2K in 2017**.
- Annual profit increased from approximately **$49.5K to $93.4K**.
- **Technology** and **Office Supplies** delivered the strongest profit margins.
- **Furniture** had significantly lower profitability and requires margin improvement.
- **Canon imageCLASS** was a major high-performing product.
- **Cubify Cube 3D Printers** created significant product-level losses.
- **California** was the highest-sales state.
- **Texas** generated high sales but recorded the largest state-level loss.
- **West and East** were major contributors to overall business performance.
- **Standard Class** generated the highest sales and profit contribution.

## 💼 Business Recommendations

1. Focus sales and marketing efforts on high-profit product categories.
2. Review pricing and discount strategies for loss-making products.
3. Investigate and improve the profitability of the Furniture category.
4. Review operations and pricing in loss-making states such as Texas.
5. Strengthen inventory allocation in high-performing regions.
6. Retain and develop high-value customers.
7. Improve inventory planning using historical sales trends.
8. Optimize shipping and logistics decisions based on profitability.
